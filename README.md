# Sales Revenue Analysis – Croma Retail Finance Insights

## Overview

This project delivers a finance analytics solution for monthly product sales revenue analysis at **Croma**, India’s leading electronics retailer. The system aggregates and analyzes sales data at the product level, aligned with Croma’s fiscal year (September to August). It empowers the finance team to monitor product performance, calculate gross revenue, and support data-driven decisions for revenue management, forecasting, and compliance.

## Industry Context

In the electronics retail sector, financial teams require precise and timely sales revenue insights to:

- Recognize revenue and close financial periods accurately
- Drive budgeting and forecasting processes
- Analyze product and category profitability
- Ensure regulatory compliance and audit readiness

Automated sales data aggregation is essential for maintaining reporting consistency and supporting rapid business decisions in a dynamic retail environment.

## Objectives

- Aggregate product sales revenue on a monthly basis, aligned with the fiscal year.
- Provide key financial metrics such as total sold quantity and gross sales value.
- Enable detailed analysis at the product and variant level for finance and merchandising teams.
- Support finance in revenue tracking, margin analysis, and strategic planning.

## Applications and Relevance

- **Revenue Management:** Track and reconcile sales revenue by product and period for accurate P&L statements.
- **Profitability Analysis:** Identify high-performing products and optimize product portfolios.
- **Financial Forecasting:** Support rolling forecasts and scenario planning with up-to-date sales data.
- **Audit & Compliance:** Ensure traceable, consistent reporting for internal and external audits.
- **Inventory & Supply Chain:** Align financial data with inventory management for better cash flow and stock control.

## Data Sources

- **Sales Transactions:** Detailed records of product sales, including product identifiers, quantities sold, and transaction dates.
- **Product Master Data:** Comprehensive information on product names and variants for enriched reporting.
- **Pricing Data:** Standardized gross price information for each product, supporting accurate revenue calculations.

## Insights

- **Seasonal Sales Patterns:** Identified clear monthly trends and seasonality in product sales, aiding in demand forecasting.
- **Top Revenue Drivers:** Highlighted products and variants contributing most to overall revenue, supporting targeted marketing and inventory decisions.
- **Variant Performance:** Revealed which product variants are most popular, informing assortment and procurement strategies.
- **Revenue Concentration:** Found that a small subset of products accounts for a significant share of total sales revenue.
- **Pricing Impact:** Analyzed how changes in gross price affect sales volume and revenue.
- **Data Quality Improvements:** Addressed issues such as duplicate entries and inconsistent pricing, resulting in more reliable financial reporting.

## What I Learned & Applied

- **SQL Joins:** Integrated data from multiple sources to create a unified sales dataset.
- **Fiscal Year Logic:** Implemented fiscal month and year calculations to match Croma’s September–August financial calendar.
- **Data Aggregation:** Used grouping and aggregation to analyze sales at the product and monthly level.
- **Data Quality Handling:** Managed duplicate products and price variations using aggregation functions and robust join logic.
- **Business Alignment:** Ensured analytics outputs meet the finance team’s requirements for reporting, audit, and compliance.
- **Performance Optimization:** Developed efficient queries to handle large-scale retail data and deliver timely insights for financial decision-making.

## Implementation

- **SQL-based aggregation** for high performance and scalability.
- **Automated reporting** to reduce manual intervention and errors.
- **Ready for integration** with BI tools and financial dashboards used by Croma’s finance team.

## Conclusion

This project provides a finance-focused, automated solution for monthly product sales revenue analysis at Croma. By aligning with fiscal periods and integrating key data sources, it empowers Croma’s finance team to make informed, timely decisions and maintain compliance with industry standards in the retail sector.
